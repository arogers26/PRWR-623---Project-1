# Procedure 

## Prerequisites
Students should already have a clear understanding of how to: 
* Access and use OneDrive 
* Write a grant proposal draft in OneDrive
* Search and identify grant opportunities
* Locate RFPs on a foundation’s website
* Login to grant portals
> [!Note]
> Students should also have access to Google Sheets.

# Step-by-Step Process

## Part 1: Making the Title
>[!Note]
> Open Google Sheets Document.

1. Select row “1A” through “1K” by clicking on “1A” and dragging the cursor to “1K”.
2. Merge cells “1A” through “1K” by clicking on the “Merge Cells” toggle above cell “1I” on the menus list. 
> [!Note] 
> This feature looks like a box with an arrow on both the left and right side that points towards the center.
3. After merging these cells, open a new tab on your computer browser and search for the website of your partnering nonprofit. 
4. Once you click on the website, move your cursor to the link at the top of the screen that starts with “https://”. Click to the right of the link so that the link is highlighted in blue then copy it. 
> [!Note] 
> Press keys “ctrl” and “C” on your keyboard to copy the link. 
5. Switch back to your google sheet tab and double click in the merged cells “1A” through “1K” space and paste the link into the Google sheet. 
> [!Note] 
> Press “ctrl” and “V” on your keyboard to paste link. 
6. Select any cell outside of “1A” through “1K”. Your link will then turn blue. When your link turns blue, hover your cursor over the link until you see three toggles appear. 
> [!Note] 
> Click on the middle one that looks like a pencil and says, “Edit Link”.
7. A tab will appear that has two links. The top link will be selected and highlighted in blue. You will press “backspace” on your keyboard to delete the top link. Then enter the title of your nonprofit organization, Ex: “Neighborhood Companions, Inc. (NCI)” and select “Apply”. 
8. To center the title, you will click the “Horizontal Align” option on the top menu that’s to the right of the “Merge Cell” toggle. Once you select it, click on the middle option to center your title.  

## Part 2: Categorizing Data
1. In cell “2A” type the words “Semester and year”
2. In cell “2B” type “Funder/Grant”
3. In cell “2C” type the words “Application Draft”
4. In cell “2D” type “Grant Author(s) & Graduate (G) or Undergraduate (U)/Email”
5. In cell “2E” type “Program/Project”
6. In cell “2F” type the words “Amount Requested”
7. In cell “2G” type “Deadline/Date pending”
8. In cell “2H” type the words “Status: Researching, In Progress, Drafted, Pending, Awarded, Declined” 
9. In cell “2I” type the word “Notes”
10. In cell “2J” type the words “Amount Awarded”
11. In cell “2K” type “Priority 1-5”

## Part 3: Adding Key Information 
1. In cell “3A” type the current semester and year you are writing your grant. (Ex: “fall 2026”)
2. Before typing anything into cell “3B”, open a new tab and search up the website of the foundation you are applying to and locate their RFP. Then move your cursor to the link at the top of the screen that starts with “https://”. Click to the right of the link so that the link is highlighted in blue. Press keys “ctrl” and “C” on your keyboard to copy the link. Return to your Google Sheet and click on “3B”. Once it is selected press the keys “ctrl” and “V” on your keyboard to paste the link. Click any cell outside of “3B” so the link will turn blue. When your link turns blue, hover your cursor over the link until you see three toggles appear. 
> [!Note] 
> Click on the middle one that looks like a pencil and says, “Edit Link”. Hit the backspace to erase the blue highlighted section and then type in the title of the foundation. (Ex: “The John J. Leidy Foundation”)
3. Before entering anything into cell "3C", open a new tab with your OneDrive documents. Locate the document you wrote your grant proposal in. Click on it and then click to the right of the link that says, “https://” so that the link is highlighted in blue. 
> [!Note]
> Press "ctrl" and "C" on keyboard to copy. Then return to Google Sheet and double click on cell "3C". After press "ctrl" and "V" to paste. Click any other cell beside "3C" and then click "3C" again. An option will pop up saying "Replace URL" and you will click the oval that says, "Chip" on the right.
4. In cell “3D” type your name, whether you are a graduate/undergraduate student, and your personal email.
5. In cell “3E” type which program you are requesting funding for to benefit your nonprofit. Ex: “General Operating”.
6. In cell “3F” type in the amount of money you are requesting from this specific funder. (Ex: $10,000)
7. Before typing anything into cell “3G”, return to the other tab where you searched up the foundations RFP and locate the grant deadline. Once you find the deadline on the RFP, return to Google Sheet and enter the date and time into “3G”. (Ex: Friday, October 16th at 11:59pm)
8. Select cell "3H" then click on the word that says "Data" toward the top left corner. A drop-down menu will appear and you will click on "Data validation". On the right side a tab will appear and you will select "+ Add Rule". You will then see where it is says, "Option 1". Backspace "Option 1" and replace it by typing the word "Researching". To the left of "Researching" you will see a white circle. Click on the circle to reveal a drop-down menu of colors. Then pick a color you would like to associate with the word "Researching". Repeat this step until the words "Researching", "In Progress", "Drafted", "Pending", "Awarded", "Declined" are on the drop-down menu with their own individual colors. Then click the word "Done".
> [!Note]
> Click the box that says, "Add another item" until you have a total of six (one for each grant status).
9. In cell "3I" put any notes that are important and relevant for specific grant. (Ex: what the funder prioritizes, important forms you need for grant, other essential dates like webinars for information, and LOI deadlines)
10. In cell "3J" type in the amount of money you have received from the funder.
> [!Note]
> If request is still pending, leave this blank until further notified by funder.
11. In cell “3K” type a number 1-5 to suggest the importance of the grant. Determine if it is the grant you will focus on first (“1”) or if you will prioritize other grants first (“5”).

## Verifying Completion
After completing all the listed steps above, you should have a grant tracking spreadsheet that includes all these components:
* Title with link to organization
* Categories for all the important grant aspects
* Link to the RFP you are applying for
* Embeded link to your drafted proposal
* Your name, education status, and personal email
* The program and amount of money you are requesting
* Proposal Deadline
* Drop down menu that suggests status of grant proposal
* Notes for grant
* Amount of money you were awarded
* Grant priority (1-5)
