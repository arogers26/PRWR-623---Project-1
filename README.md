# PRWR 623 - Project 1: Creating Grant Tracking Documentation
## Introduction
It is imperative for grant writers to be mindful and aware of grant requirements, proposal status, application deadlines, and information about funders. If a grant writer loses this vital information, it will be detrimental to the nonprofit they are partnering with because they will miss funding opportunities. Since nonprofits rely on grant funding from foundations, these opportunities are crucial.

## Audience
This guide will help students enrolled in ENGL 401 (Grant and Advocacy Writing) and PRWR 619 (Grant and Community Writing) create documentation on Google Sheets that sort and categorize grant tracking information. These instructions are intended for students who have already developed a strong understanding of grant writing and are confident in their abilities to identify grant opportunities. Therefore, this manual will be introduced to ENGL 401 and PRWR 619 students later in their academic semester. 

## Purpose
Through these instructions students will learn how to:
- Organize and compare various grant opportunities 
- Understand the importance of placing all essential grant writing details into one single source
- Identify due dates and stay on top of their work
- Avoid missing important deadlines for grants
- Manage and update the status of their grant proposal
