# Troubleshooting

## Uploading Proposal Draft
When uploading your draft to your grant tracking documentation, ensure that you are using your personal Gmail account, do not use your student outlook account. Your OneDrive should be connected to your Gmail instead of your Towson Outlook e-mail.

## How to Arrange Deadlines on Grant Tracking
* Put the most recent grants you are working on toward the top of the grant tracker
* Place submitted proposals closer to the bottom of the tracker
* Organize proposals by their due date (Ex: spring 2026 grants should be under fall 2026)
